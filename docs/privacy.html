Privacy Policy for HardDiskSpace

Last updated: April 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HardDiskSpace ("we," "the app," or "us") handles information when you use our application, available through the Microsoft Store.

Summary

HardDiskSpace does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data. The app runs entirely on your local device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any information from you. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • Account or login credentials
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports
  • Device identifiers
  • Location data
  • Files or documents you scan or analyze with the app

Data Storage

Any data HardDiskSpace generates while analyzing your disk (such as scan results, file listings, or size reports) is stored locally on your device under your control. We do not have access to this data, and it is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

HardDiskSpace does not use any third-party services, including analytics providers, advertising networks, cloud services, or tracking tools.

Purchases

Purchases of HardDiskSpace are processed by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store. We do not receive or have access to your payment information. For details on how Microsoft handles purchase data, please refer to the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Children's Privacy

HardDiskSpace does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
