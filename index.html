About HardDiskSpace

HardDiskSpace was built around a simple idea: finding out what's filling your hard drive shouldn't take longer than freeing the space.

The app scans quickly, runs entirely on your local machine, and never sends your data anywhere. No accounts, no telemetry, no upsells.

  • Fast, multi-threaded disk scanning
  • Visual breakdown of folder sizes
  • Identify large files in seconds
  • 100% local — your data stays on your device
  • No ads, no tracking, no accounts
